AudioGraph demo

Seven charts of made-up data for a small town called Riverton: line graphs, bar charts and a pie chart. Each one can be played as sound.

  • Press Play under any chart. The first time, it reads out what the chart shows (each word is highlighted as it is spoken), then plays it.
  • Line graphs are one tone that glides with the line. Bar and pie charts are different: each bar or slice is a separate note, played in turn, with its name spoken. (Pie slices also say their share.)
  • In all of them, the lowest value is a low note, the median (the middle value) is always the same note, and the highest is a high note.
  • A short ting sounds when the values cross the median: a higher ting going up, a lower one going down.
  • Headphones help with the two comparisons, where two tones play at once.

Line graphs

One tone glides up and down with the line, taking 15 seconds however many values there are.
